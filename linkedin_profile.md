### **About**

For over two decades, I've navigated the dynamic landscape of software engineering, from the structured, often challenging, world of enterprise systems to the agile, open frontier of the web, and now, to the cutting edge of AI and immersive technologies. My journey is defined by a relentless curiosity, a passion for building, and a deep-seated belief in the power of open collaboration.

My early career, spent wrestling with the complexities of proprietary platforms like Documentum and WebSphere, instilled in me a profound appreciation for robust architecture, alongside a healthy skepticism for closed systems. This experience directly fueled my advocacy for open source, a philosophy that has guided my technical choices and contributions ever since.

In 2018, I made a deliberate pivot, transitioning from a rewarding role leading innovation at a major tech company to embrace the life of a digital nomad and independent product builder. This shift was driven by a desire for autonomy, a focus on deep work, and the freedom to pursue projects that align with my core values. I'm now dedicated to building my own SaaS products, contributing to the open-source community, and exploring the practical applications of AI and immersive technologies.

I thrive on solving complex problems, learning new paradigms, and sharing my insights. My work is a continuous experiment in building, optimizing, and documenting the process—from technical deep-dives and product strategy to the practicalities of remote work and the philosophical underpinnings of a life lived in code.

I'm always eager to connect with fellow builders, innovators, and those passionate about shaping the future of technology. Let's explore what's possible.
